GRAVITY BUDGET — ENGLISH TEXT FOR DHIVEHI TRANSLATION
=====================================================

How to fill this in:
- Write your Dhivehi after the "=" on the line under each English text.
- Leave the [code] in square brackets as it is (it tells the app where the text goes).
- Keep anything in {{double braces}} exactly as it is, e.g. {{amount}}, {{name}}, {{count}}, {{date}}.
  You can move it anywhere in your sentence. {{amount}} becomes a money amount like "Rf 1,250.00".
- Lines ending in _one are for exactly 1 (e.g. "1 day"); _other is for 2 or more.
- Leave a line empty to keep the Dhivehi the app shows now.
- Brand names stay in English: Gravity Budget, PDF, Dhiraagu, STELCO.
