# Hunaru (ހުނަރު) — gig pay app for Maldivian freelancers

Status: idea + clickable demo (docs/marketing/hunaru-demo/hunaru-demo.html). Not built.

## The problem
Maldivian designers, video editors, translators, photographers and developers find work on Instagram and Viber.
Clients worry about paying first and getting nothing; freelancers worry about working first and not getting paid.
There is no local place to show your work, agree a price and get paid safely in MVR.

## How it works
1. **Freelancer** makes gigs: e.g. "Logo design: Basic Rf 500 / Standard Rf 900 / Premium Rf 1,500", with
   delivery days, revisions and portfolio pictures.
2. **Client** picks a gig and package and pays by bank transfer (slip upload). The money is **held** by Hunaru.
3. Freelancer delivers files in the order chat. Client approves, or asks for a revision.
4. On approval (or automatically 3 days after delivery with no reply) the money is **released** to the freelancer,
   minus the fee. Freelancer withdraws to their bank account.
5. Problem? Either side opens a dispute; the Hunaru team decides from the chat and files.

## Screens
- Client: browse by category, gig page with packages, order + pay, order chat, approve / revision, rate.
- Freelancer: my gigs, orders (to do, delivered, done), deliver files, earnings (held / available), withdraw.
- Super Admin: payments to confirm, releases, withdrawals, disputes.

## Money
- Fee: 10% from the freelancer on each completed order (5% when the client is a business on a monthly plan).
- Businesses: MVR 300/month plan for teams that hire often (one invoice, lower fees).
- Featured gigs (paid boost).

## Must check before building (important)
- Holding client money ("escrow") may need permission from the Maldives Monetary Authority, or a partner bank /
  licensed payment provider holding the funds. Get advice first. A first version can avoid holding money:
  the client pays the freelancer directly and Hunaru only records the agreement, delivery and approval.
- Tax: freelancer income and TGST rules for the platform fee.

## Why now
No local version exists as far as we know (check App Store / Facebook first). Fiverr and Upwork pay in USD,
take high fees and are hard for MVR clients.
