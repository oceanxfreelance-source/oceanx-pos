# Darani (ދަރަނި) — credit book for shops and their customers

Status: idea + clickable demo (docs/marketing/darani-demo/darani-demo.html). Not built.

## The problem
In Malé and on the islands, many people buy groceries, food and fuel on credit at their local shop and pay
on salary day. Shops write it in a notebook. Credit is forgotten, customers dispute amounts, and shops wait
for money they are owed.

## Two sides, one app
**Shop (every day)**
- Write a credit sale in seconds (amount, note, photo of the bill) — or it comes in from OceanX POS credit sales.
- Credit limit per customer; warning before going over.
- See who owes what, oldest first; total owed to the shop.
- One tap: send salary-day reminders (Viber text with the customer's balance and a link).
- Take payment (cash / transfer with slip); receipt sent to the customer.

**Customer (every month, checked often)**
- One list: what I owe at every shop, and the total.
- Every purchase as it happens (no surprises at month end).
- Pay: transfer and upload the slip; the shop confirms.
- Salary-day reminder; links to Gravity Budget ("Shops I owe" as part of the month).

## Why it spreads
Shops pay; customers are free. Each shop brings its credit customers into the app (often 50–300 people).
Customers who use it ask their other shops to join.

## Links to OceanX
- OceanX POS: credit sales flow in automatically (already has customers + credit add-on).
- Theyo Fihaara: fuel on credit for boats and contractors.
- Gravity Budget: the person's debts to shops appear in their month.

## Pricing idea
- Shop: MVR 150 / month (up to 100 customers), MVR 300 (unlimited). First month free.
- Customer: free.

## Rules
- A customer sees only their own balance with each shop; a shop sees only its own customers.
- The customer must accept the link to a shop (phone number + one-time code) before seeing it.
- Disputes: a customer can flag a line; the shop sees it.
