# Fathuruverin (ފަތުރުވެރިން) — guesthouse manager (on hold)

Status: idea + demo done, not built. Owner said "keep this in mind" (2026-10-25).

- Logo: docs/marketing/fathuruverin-demo/fathuruverin-logo.png (the corrected one).
- Demo pictures (desktop, professional style, SAMPLE data): docs/marketing/fathuruverin-demo/
- Mostly English, second language Dhivehi. Used mainly on PC; owner will say which parts go to the phone.

## Positioning
"Other systems manage rooms. Fathuruverin manages the whole guest journey on a local island:
flight, speedboat, room, trips, café and taxes."

Target: guesthouses with 5–25 rooms that run transfers, excursions and a café (Maafushi, Thulusdhoo, Dhigurah, Fulidhoo...).

## Key features
1. Flight tracking → speedboat auto-reschedule → captain/guest/kitchen notified
2. Transfers: boats, seats, captains, shared transfers
3. Excursions with seats, guides, partner commissions, on the bill
4. Café/restaurant via OceanX POS, charged to room
5. Staff screens in 6 languages (en, dv, bn, hi, ne, si); works offline
6. TGST + Green Tax on invoices (rates as settings), USD + MVR
7. Own booking website (no commission); booking-site calendar sync first, two-way later

## Competitors checked (from their websites, Oct 2026 — verify by trying them)
- Fenuva: $39/mo ≤10 rooms, +$5/room, $100 setup, channel manager +$33.99; English only; needs internet.
- Vayves: $19/$49/$79, 30-day trial; iCal sync Booking.com/Airbnb; BML Connect; claims 934 guesthouses.
- Frontdesko: Free (PMS only), Rf 640 (11–30 rooms), Rf 825 (31–50); many OTAs; Green Tax; page says TGST 16%.
- Qvian: closest rival — rooms + full café POS + excursions + double-entry accounting; price not shown.

## Pricing idea
Rf 499 (≤10 rooms) / Rf 899 (≤25 rooms), free setup and data move, channels included.

## Payments
No gateway needed to launch: subscriptions by bank transfer + slip (existing billing);
guest payments recorded (transfer slip, card machine, cash, pay at property, OTA prepaid).
Later: each guesthouse connects its own BML Connect merchant account.

2. Try Vayves, Frontdesko free plans; demos of Fenuva and Qvian.
3. Confirm current TGST / Green Tax rates with MIRA.
